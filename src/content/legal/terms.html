6. Blockchain & Permanence

A. What blockchain technology is

A blockchain is a shared digital ledger kept by a network of independent computers. Each new record is linked to the records before it and copied across the network, so once it is confirmed it cannot be quietly changed or removed by any single party, including Auvra. Anyone can view the records on a public blockchain.

B. How Auvra uses blockchain technology

Auvra uses a public blockchain network for two purposes:

  • Permanent records of badges. When a Creator Badge or Contribution Badge is preserved, a small record is written to the blockchain. It shows when the badge was created, who created it and that its content has not been altered since, so attribution and provenance can be verified independently of Auvra.
  • Peer-to-peer payments. Claims of Creator Badges and purchases of physical artifacts are paid in USDC directly between users' Auvra wallets, as described in Section 5.

Auvra chooses the public blockchain network it uses and may change or add networks over time to keep records secure, affordable and durable. Records already written remain on the network where they were created.

Badges on Auvra are records of cultural attribution and provenance. They are not investment products, securities or currencies, and Auvra does not promise that any badge will hold or gain value.

C. What is recorded on the blockchain

The on-chain record of a badge contains only: the badge ID, the creator's wallet address, a timestamp and a metadata hash (a digital fingerprint of the badge's content). This data becomes immutable once preserved and cannot be deleted or altered. Full media content (images, audio, video) is stored off-chain using decentralised storage networks, and is linked to the badge by its metadata hash.

D. What is not recorded on the blockchain

Your name, email address, phone number, identity documents and other account information are never written to the blockchain. Content stored in My Vault (private family memories, photos, voice recordings, documents) is not recorded on the public blockchain. It is encrypted and stored off-chain. You control access via sharing permissions and authentication, and you can delete this content at any time.

E. What this means for you

  • Permanence: on-chain badge records are permanent by design and cannot be edited or removed, even by Auvra.
  • Public visibility: on-chain records and wallet addresses can be seen by anyone. A wallet address does not show your name, but it could be linked to you through other information.
  • Irreversible payments: USDC payments cannot be cancelled or reversed by Auvra once confirmed on the blockchain.
  • Network risks: blockchain networks are run by third parties. They may experience delays, fees, outages or changes to their rules that Auvra does not control (see Section 8).

F. Deletion Limitations

Because blockchain records are immutable, we cannot delete or alter on-chain badge data once preserved. If you request deletion of your account, we will remove off-chain content and personal information where possible, but on-chain badge records will remain as part of the public cultural archive. We will not associate them with your identity after deletion.
