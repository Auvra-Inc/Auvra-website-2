D. Phygital Items (Physical Artifacts with Digital Twins)

Auvra allows creators to pair a physical artifact (such as a sculpture, textile, or painting) with a digital Creator Badge (the "Digital Twin").

  • The Digital Twin serves exclusively as a Certificate of Authenticity and Provenance for the physical artifact. It is not a standalone digital collectible.
  • The purchase of a physical artifact via the Auvra platform includes the transfer of the associated Digital Twin. The price displayed is for the physical item; the Digital Twin is provided as a non-separable record of ownership.
  • Payment for the physical artifact occurs via the buyer's USDC wallet (peer-to-peer). Because this is a transaction for a physical good, it is not subject to In-App Purchase (IAP) under Apple or Google's.
  • Auvra deducts a 5% platform fee from the creator's proceeds for facilitating this transaction.
  • The Digital Twin Badge is a passive record of authenticity. It does not grant the user any 'premium' features, digital content, or enhanced functionality within the Auvra mobile application. Its sole purpose is the immutable recording of provenance for the associated physical object.

6. Blockchain & Permanence

A. On-Chain Records

Creator and Contribution Badges are recorded on a public blockchain. The following data becomes immutable and cannot be deleted or altered once preserved: badge ID, creator wallet address, timestamp, and metadata hash. Full media content (images, audio, video) is stored off-chain using decentralised storage networks.

B. Private Vault Content

Content stored in My Vault (private family memories, photos, voice recordings, documents) is not recorded on the public blockchain. It is encrypted and stored off-chain. You control access via sharing permissions and authentication. This content can be deleted by you at any time.

C. Deletion Limitations

Because blockchain records are immutable, we cannot delete or alter on-chain badge data once preserved. If you request deletion of your account, we will remove off-chain content and personal information where possible, but on-chain badge records will remain as part of the public cultural archive. We will not associate them with your identity after deletion.
