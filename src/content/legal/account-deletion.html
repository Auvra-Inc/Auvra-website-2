1. Introduction

This Account Deletion Policy (the “Policy”) sets out the procedures and conditions governing requests to delete an Auvra account. It explains how account deletion may be requested, how requests are verified and processed, the categories of information that may be deleted, and the information that Auvra may retain following account closure.

Auvra is operated by Auvra Tech Ltd, a company registered in Nigeria (RC Number: 8895504) (“Auvra”, “we”, “us” or “our”).

Auvra respects the privacy rights of its users and is committed to processing personal data in accordance with applicable data protection legislation, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (“NDPA”) and other applicable laws.

Account deletion is intended to terminate the user’s account and remove eligible personal information from Auvra’s systems. However, deletion is subject to legal retention obligations, legitimate grounds for continued processing, the rights of other individuals, technical limitations and the characteristics of public blockchain networks.

Accordingly, account deletion does not necessarily result in the removal of every record associated with a user, particularly records permanently published to a public blockchain.

This Policy forms part of Auvra’s privacy and data governance framework and should be read together with the Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, AI Policy, AML/CFT Policy and other applicable policies.

2. Scope and Important Definitions

For the purposes of this Policy:

Account Deletion Request means a request submitted by a user or an authorised representative seeking the closure of an Auvra account and deletion of eligible personal information associated with that account.

Active Systems means Auvra’s live production systems used to operate its applications, websites and services.

Personal Data (also referred to in this Policy as “personal information”) means information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, as defined under applicable data protection law.

Public Blockchain Records means information recorded on a public blockchain, including relevant wallet addresses, transaction records, timestamps, badge identifiers, ownership records and metadata hashes.

Restricted Backups means backup, disaster-recovery and archival copies maintained for security, business continuity or recovery purposes and not ordinarily used to operate users’ accounts.

Retained Information means personal information Auvra is required or otherwise lawfully permitted to preserve following account deletion.

Anonymisation means the irreversible transformation of information so that an individual can no longer reasonably be identified, directly or indirectly, having regard to the information and other reasonably available data.

Information that has merely been separated from a person’s name or account identifier is not necessarily anonymised. Pseudonymised information, including a wallet address that can still be linked to an individual, remains subject to applicable personal data protection requirements.

3. Deletion at a Glance

Users may request account deletion through the Auvra application or by contacting Auvra directly.

Following a verified request:

  • Auvra will close the account and commence the deletion process.
  • Eligible personal information will be deleted from Active Systems within the operational period specified in this Policy.
  • Information that Auvra must retain for legal, regulatory, financial, security or dispute-related purposes will be restricted to the relevant retention purpose.
  • Restricted Backups will be dealt with under Auvra’s documented backup-retention procedures.
  • Public Blockchain Records will generally remain permanently accessible.
  • Information independently held by third parties, or copies previously downloaded or shared outside Auvra, may remain beyond Auvra’s control.

Account deletion does not reverse completed transactions, automatically cancel external subscriptions or guarantee the removal of information from public blockchains.

4. Before Requesting Account Deletion

Users should consider the following before submitting an Account Deletion Request:

  1. Save any personal memories, photographs, documents, recordings or other materials stored in My Vault that they wish to preserve.
  2. Save copies of important information or cultural materials they have contributed to public or community records.
  3. Review active Heirloom Badge listings, claims, transactions and other outstanding activities.
  4. Transfer any USDC held in their Auvra wallet to another wallet they control, as they may lose access to the wallet through Auvra once the account is deleted.
  5. Resolve pending payments, disputes or contractual obligations where possible.
  6. Cancel subscriptions separately through the relevant payment provider.
  7. Consider the permanent nature of Public Blockchain Records, including wallet addresses, metadata hashes and transaction histories.
  8. Understand that deletion is intended to be permanent and that deleted private content may not be recoverable.

Where Auvra provides a download or export function for particular content, users may use that function before requesting deletion. Where such functionality is unavailable, users should independently save copies through any available application functionality.

Auvra does not represent that every category of account information is exportable.

5. How to Request Account Deletion

5.1 Through the Auvra Application

Users may request account deletion directly through the Auvra mobile application by following these steps:

  1. Open the Auvra application.
  2. Navigate to Settings.
  3. Select Account.
  4. Choose Delete Account.
  5. Review the deletion notice, including the consequences for private content, subscriptions, badges and blockchain records.
  6. Confirm the deletion request.
  7. Complete any identity-verification requirements presented by the application.

Auvra may send an acknowledgement to the email address associated with the account.

An acknowledgement confirms receipt only. It does not necessarily mean that the request has been verified, accepted or completed.

5.2 Where the User Cannot Access the Account

A user who cannot access their account or complete the in-app deletion process may submit a request by email:

Email: info@goauvra.com
Subject: Account Deletion Request

The request should include the email address associated with the account and sufficient information to enable Auvra to identify the relevant account.

Auvra will acknowledge email requests and communicate any additional verification requirements.

Users should exercise caution when responding to communications concerning account deletion. Auvra will not request account passwords, private wallet keys or wallet recovery phrases.

5.3 No Unnecessary Barriers

Auvra will not require a user to provide information that is irrelevant or disproportionate to the verification of a deletion request.

Auvra will not require a user to explain why they wish to leave the platform as a condition of exercising their deletion rights.

6. Verification and Processing of Requests

Auvra will take reasonable and proportionate steps to verify the identity of the requester and protect accounts against unauthorised deletion.

Depending on the circumstances, verification may include:

  • Confirmation through the registered email address.
  • A verification code sent through an established account communication channel.
  • Confirmation through an authenticated application session.
  • An account identifier or relevant account information.
  • Limited account or transaction information where reasonably necessary.
  • Evidence of legal authority where a representative submits the request.

Auvra will collect and process only the information reasonably necessary for verification.

Where a request is incomplete or identity cannot reasonably be established, Auvra may request additional information before proceeding.

Where Auvra refuses or limits a request, it will, subject to legal restrictions, explain the reason and inform the requester of the available means of seeking reconsideration or lodging a complaint.

Once a request has been verified and accepted, Auvra will commence the account closure and deletion process.

7. What Happens to Personal Information?

Upon acceptance of a verified Account Deletion Request, Auvra will delete personal information that is no longer required for a lawful purpose and that is not subject to an applicable retention exception.

Subject to those requirements, the information deleted may include:

  • Account profile information, including name, email address and telephone number.
  • Profile photographs and personal account preferences.
  • Personal account settings and associated identifiers.
  • Private content stored in My Vault.
  • Personal messages and associated account information, where applicable.
  • Uploaded files and associated off-chain metadata.
  • Account-to-wallet associations maintained within Auvra’s systems.
  • Other personal information for which Auvra has no continuing lawful basis to retain or process.

Auvra will take reasonable technical and organisational steps to remove eligible information from Active Systems within 30 calendar days after a verified Account Deletion Request has been accepted for processing.

This commitment applies to eligible information in Active Systems. It does not override lawful retention obligations or guarantee the removal of Public Blockchain Records, Restricted Backups or information held independently by third parties.

Where information cannot be deleted immediately from a particular system, Auvra will restrict its use where appropriate and apply its documented retention and deletion procedures.

8. My Vault and Private Content

My Vault is Auvra’s private preservation space for personal memories, cultural materials and collections.

Upon acceptance of an Account Deletion Request, eligible My Vault content will be scheduled for deletion as part of the account deletion process.

Auvra will:

  • Delete eligible private materials stored within My Vault.
  • Remove associated personal information and off-chain content, subject to applicable retention requirements.
  • Prevent deleted private materials from being used for ordinary platform operations.
  • Ensure that private materials in My Vault are not automatically converted into public content as a consequence of account deletion.

Users are responsible for independently saving materials they wish to preserve before completing deletion.

Content previously downloaded, exported, shared or stored outside Auvra’s systems may remain available to the individuals or services holding those copies. Auvra cannot guarantee the removal of such independently held copies.

9. Shared and Community Contributions

Auvra supports the preservation of cultural heritage through individual, family, community and creator contributions.

Certain materials may be jointly created, shared, maintained or contributed by multiple individuals. Some may form part of a community collection or a public cultural record.

An individual’s account deletion will not automatically require the deletion of materials independently contributed by, owned by or lawfully maintained for other users.

Where a deletion request concerns shared or collaborative content, Auvra will assess:

  • The personal information relating to the departing user.
  • The nature and ownership of the underlying content.
  • The rights and interests of other contributors.
  • Any applicable intellectual property or contractual rights.
  • Whether the content forms part of an established public or community cultural record.
  • Whether continued retention is supported by a lawful basis.

Where the departing user is the sole contributor, Auvra will assess the request separately and will not automatically treat the material as content belonging to other users.

Where deletion of the user’s personal information is required, Auvra will take appropriate steps to remove or de-identify that information from off-chain systems, subject to applicable law and technical limitations.

Where a contribution has already been permanently recorded on a public blockchain, account deletion will not ordinarily remove the underlying blockchain record.

Nothing in this section authorises Auvra to retain personal information merely because it forms part of a cultural contribution. Any continued processing must have an appropriate lawful basis.

10. Contribution Badges and Heirloom Badges

Auvra operates two public badge systems:

Contribution Badges recognise cultural and communal contributions, including contributions made through the available cultural contribution paths.

Heirloom Badges represent cultural and creative expressions made available for discovery and claiming.

Account deletion does not automatically erase badges, ownership records or transactions that have already been permanently recorded on a public blockchain.

10.1 Contribution Badges

Where a Contribution Badge has been recorded on a public blockchain:

  • The blockchain record may remain permanently accessible.
  • The badge identifier, associated wallet address, timestamp and metadata hash may remain visible.
  • Existing provenance and contribution records may remain available.
  • Eligible personal information held separately by Auvra will be deleted or de-identified in accordance with this Policy.

10.2 Heirloom Badges

Where an account is associated with an Heirloom Badge:

  • Completed transactions will not be reversed solely because the account is deleted.
  • Existing blockchain ownership and provenance records may remain permanently accessible.
  • Outstanding claims, transactions, disputes or contractual obligations will continue to be governed by the Terms of Service and applicable agreements.
  • Eligible off-chain personal information will be deleted or de-identified in accordance with this Policy.

The treatment of active listings, uncompleted claims and other pending badge activities will follow Auvra’s applicable transaction and content-governance procedures.

Account deletion does not guarantee the removal of an Heirloom Badge or Contribution Badge from a public blockchain.

11. Public Blockchain Records and Data Permanence

Auvra uses blockchain technology to support the integrity, provenance and verification of cultural records.

Users acknowledge that information recorded on a public blockchain may be replicated across independent network participants and may not be capable of being deleted or modified by Auvra.

11.1 On-Chain Information

Depending on the relevant badge and transaction, Public Blockchain Records may include:

  • Badge identifiers.
  • Public wallet addresses.
  • Transaction hashes.
  • Transaction timestamps.
  • Metadata hashes.
  • Ownership and provenance entries.
  • Other technical information necessary to record or verify a transaction.

A public wallet address or transaction record may constitute personal data where it can reasonably be associated with an identifiable individual.

Auvra does not treat information as anonymous merely because a person’s name is not written directly to a blockchain.

11.2 Off-Chain Information

Information associated with a badge may also be stored outside the blockchain, including photographs, audio recordings, video, documents, descriptions and supporting cultural materials.

Such information may be capable of deletion or de-identification, depending on the storage architecture, applicable legal obligations and the rights of other individuals.

Where Auvra controls the relevant off-chain storage, it will process eligible deletion requests in accordance with this Policy.

11.3 Metadata Hashes

A metadata hash is a cryptographic representation used to verify the integrity of associated information.

Where a metadata hash has already been recorded on a public blockchain, deleting the underlying off-chain file will not delete the hash.

The hash may remain permanently accessible and may be used to verify another copy of the associated information if one exists.

Users should therefore understand that deletion of a photograph, recording or document from Auvra does not necessarily erase its previously published cryptographic reference.

11.4 Wallet Addresses and Linkability

Upon account deletion, Auvra will remove account-to-wallet associations from systems under its control where required and technically possible.

However, removal of an association from Auvra’s systems does not anonymise the wallet address or guarantee that the address cannot be linked to an individual.

Such identification may remain possible through transaction histories, public information, information held by third parties or blockchain analysis services.

Auvra cannot guarantee the removal of associations independently established outside its systems.

11.5 Data Minimisation Before Blockchain Publication

Auvra will apply a data-minimisation approach when designing and operating its badge and blockchain systems.

In particular:

  • Direct personal identifiers, such as names, email addresses, telephone numbers and identity documents, will not be written to public blockchains.
  • Sensitive personal information will not be included in public blockchain metadata.
  • Where verification is required, Auvra will use off-chain storage and cryptographic references rather than publishing personal information.
  • Wallet addresses and metadata hashes must be assessed for their potential to identify individuals.
  • Users should receive clear information about the permanent and public nature of the relevant records before submitting content for public blockchain publication.

Auvra will not represent that information published to a public blockchain can subsequently be erased through an ordinary account deletion request.

12. Information Auvra May Retain

Auvra may retain certain personal information after account deletion where it has a continuing lawful basis or is required to do so by applicable law.

Permitted retention purposes may include:

  • Compliance with legal and regulatory obligations.
  • KYC and AML requirements.
  • Tax, accounting and financial reporting.
  • Prevention and investigation of fraud or security incidents.
  • Resolution of disputes and outstanding transactions.
  • Establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.
  • Enforcement of contractual rights.
  • Protection of the rights, property and safety of Auvra, its users and third parties.
  • Demonstration of compliance with legal and regulatory requirements.

Retained information will be limited to the information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

Auvra will not retain personal information indefinitely merely because it was once associated with an active account.

Where the relevant retention purpose expires, Auvra will delete or anonymise the information in accordance with its retention procedures, unless another lawful basis applies.

Access to Retained Information will be restricted to authorised personnel, service providers or authorities with a legitimate need to access it.

13. Retention Periods and Backup Management

Auvra applies retention periods according to the category of information, its purpose, applicable legal requirements and operational necessity.

Eligible account informationRemoval from Active Systems within 30 calendar days after acceptance of a verified deletion request.
My Vault contentDeletion following acceptance of the request, subject to lawful retention exceptions and technical limitations.

13.1 Restricted Backups

Restricted Backups are maintained for business continuity, disaster recovery and system security.

Deleted information may remain temporarily in a backup that cannot reasonably be individually modified without compromising the integrity of the backup system.

Auvra will:

  • Restrict access to Restricted Backups.
  • Prohibit their use for ordinary platform operations or unrelated commercial purposes.
  • Apply documented backup rotation and expiry procedures.
  • Reapply applicable deletion instructions if a backup is restored into an operational environment.
  • Delete or overwrite backup copies in accordance with the applicable retention schedule.

Auvra maintains a documented maximum retention period for Restricted Backups and reviews it regularly.

Deleted information is removed from Restricted Backups when those backups expire under Auvra’s regular backup rotation cycle, as described in the Privacy Policy.

13.2 Statutory and Regulatory Records

KYC, AML, financial and transaction records will be retained for the specific periods required by the applicable legal or regulatory obligation.

KYC and AML records are kept for at least five (5) years after the date of the transaction or the end of the business relationship, whichever is later, as set out in the AML/CFT Policy. Financial and transaction records are kept for seven (7) years for tax and accounting purposes, as set out in the Privacy Policy. A longer period may apply where required by law or for an ongoing investigation or legal claim.

14. Artificial Intelligence and Model Training

Auvra may process personal information and user-generated content through AI-enabled services, including Lens AI and other cultural preservation technologies.

AI processing information may include prompts, outputs, technical logs, processing records and associated identifiers, depending on the feature used.

Auvra will apply the AI processing and retention terms described in its Privacy Policy and AI Policy.

Auvra will not use private user content or personal information to train its own AI models by default. Where model training relies on user consent, Auvra will obtain the required explicit consent before using that information for such training.

Users may withdraw consent for future processing where consent is the applicable lawful basis. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully completed before withdrawal.

Following account deletion, Auvra will delete eligible personal information contained in AI processing logs in accordance with the applicable retention schedule and legal requirements.

Where information has already been incorporated into a trained model, individual extraction or removal may not be technically feasible. This limitation does not remove Auvra’s obligation to comply with applicable data protection law or its commitments concerning consent and future model training.

15. Subscriptions, Payments and Outstanding Obligations

Account deletion does not automatically cancel subscriptions purchased through an external payment provider.

Users must separately cancel subscriptions through the relevant provider, including:

  • Apple App Store.
  • Google Play.

Deleting an account does not:

  • Reverse a completed payment.
  • Automatically entitle the user to a refund.
  • Cancel an outstanding financial or contractual obligation.
  • Reverse a completed blockchain transaction.
  • Remove a transaction record that is lawfully retained.
  • Delete Public Blockchain Records.

Refunds, transaction disputes and outstanding obligations will be handled in accordance with Auvra’s Terms of Service and the applicable payment provider’s terms.

16. Requests Made on Behalf of Minors or Other Individuals

Where permitted by applicable law, a parent, legal guardian or duly authorised representative may submit an Account Deletion Request on behalf of a user under the age of majority, or another individual who is legally incapable of submitting the request independently.

Auvra may request reasonable evidence of:

  • The identity of the requester.
  • The requester’s relationship to the account holder.
  • The requester’s legal authority to act on the account holder’s behalf.
  • Any additional information reasonably necessary to protect the account holder’s rights and interests.

Auvra will assess such requests in accordance with applicable data protection, child-protection and legal requirements.

Auvra may communicate directly with the account holder where appropriate and lawful.

17. Requests Following the Death of an Account Holder

Where an account holder has died, a verified family member, executor, administrator or other legally authorised representative may contact Auvra to request the closure of the account and the handling of personal information.

Auvra may request reasonable documentation to verify:

  • The death of the account holder.
  • The identity of the requester.
  • The requester’s legal authority or relationship to the deceased.
  • The specific action requested.

Auvra will assess the request in accordance with applicable law, the rights of other individuals, contractual obligations and the nature of the relevant information.

The closure of a deceased user’s account does not automatically transfer ownership of badges, wallet assets, cultural materials or other property to the requester.

Blockchain assets and records may be subject to separate inheritance, succession, wallet-access and contractual arrangements.

Auvra will not request or accept a deceased user’s private wallet keys or recovery phrases as proof of authority.
