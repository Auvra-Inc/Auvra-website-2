11. Blockchain & Permanence

What Blockchain Technology Means on Auvra

A blockchain is a shared digital ledger kept by a network of independent computers. Once a record is confirmed, it cannot be changed or removed by any single party, including Auvra, and anyone can view it. Auvra uses a public blockchain to keep permanent, verifiable records of Heirloom Badges and Contribution Badges, so their attribution and provenance can be checked independently, and to process USDC payments between users. Section 6 of our Terms of Service explains this in more detail.

On-Chain Records

When a badge is preserved, the following data is recorded on the blockchain and becomes immutable: badge ID, creator wallet address, timestamp and metadata hash (a digital fingerprint of the badge’s content). Full media content is stored off-chain using decentralised storage networks. We never write your name, email address, phone number, identity documents or other account information to the blockchain.

A wallet address does not show your name, but it may be personal information where it can be linked to you. We process it to provide badges and payments you request (contract performance) and to keep a reliable public cultural archive (legitimate interests).

Private Vault Content

Content stored in My Vault is not recorded on the public blockchain. It is encrypted and stored off-chain. You control access and may delete it at any time.

Deletion Limitations

On-chain records cannot be deleted or corrected, even by Auvra. When you delete your account, we remove the link between those records and your identity on Auvra, but the records themselves remain on the blockchain as part of the public cultural archive. Please consider this before preserving a badge publicly.
