1. Introduction

This Content Governance Policy explains how Auvra manages cultural content across the Platform - specifically, how content is verified, moderated, and removed when it violates our rules or legal obligations. It does not replace our Copyright Policy (legal takedowns) or Community Guidelines (user behaviour). Instead, it focuses on the operational framework for content decisions.

This policy applies to all content on the Platform, including badges, profiles, comments, collaboration files, and private vault content (where applicable).

2. Governance Tiers by Content Type

Different content types have different governance models:

Content TypeGovernance Model
Creator Works (original art, music, crafts)Creator-owned. Removed only for legal violations (e.g., copyright, illegality) or policy violations (e.g., hate speech).
Contribution BadgesCommunity-verified. Subject to authenticity review by designated community members.
Private Vault ContentUser-controlled. Auvra does not moderate unless reported or required by law.
User Profiles and CommentsSubject to Community Guidelines. Moderated for abuse, hate speech, or harassment.
Collaboration FilesGoverned by Collaboration Hub Terms. Moderated only for legal or severe policy violations.

3. Community Verification (Contribution Badges)

Contribution Badges rely on community members to verify cultural authenticity and accuracy.

Who can verify?

  • Community elders - individuals recognised by their community as having authority over cultural knowledge. Auvra does not appoint elders; communities designate them.
  • Community verifiers - users who have earned trust through consistent, accurate contributions over time, as determined by Auvra's reputation system (e.g., number of successful verifications, positive feedback).

What can be verified?

  • Cultural authenticity (e.g., "This is a genuine Igbo proverb")
  • Historical or contextual accuracy
  • Appropriate classification (e.g., sacred vs. public)

Verification process

  1. A user submits a Contribution Badge.
  2. Relevant communities are notified for verification.
  3. Verified users may approve, reject, or add contextual notes.
  4. A badge may be marked as "Community Verified" after sufficient endorsements (threshold defined operationally).
  5. Disputed badges may be escalated to community elders or Auvra moderators.

Verification thresholds

The number of endorsements required for "Community Verified" status may be adjusted based on platform growth, abuse patterns, and community feedback. Auvra will announce significant changes to verification thresholds.

Verification is not a guarantee of absolute truth it reflects reasonable consensus within the verifying community. Auvra does not arbitrate disputes over cultural truth.

4. Moderation Standards

Auvra moderates content based on these standards:

  • Illegal content - Removed and reported to authorities (e.g., child exploitation, terrorist content).
  • Hate speech - Content that attacks or demeans individuals or groups based on protected characteristics.
  • Harassment and threats - Repeated targeting, intimidation, or doxxing.
  • Cultural misrepresentation - Knowingly false statements of cultural origin or significance.
  • Spam and scams - Repetitive, irrelevant, or deceptive content.

Who moderates?

  • Automated tools (for spam, sensitive content flags)
  • Trained Auvra moderators
  • Community verifiers (for Contribution Badges)

Moderation actions may include: content removal, labelling, reduced visibility, account restriction, or suspension.

5. Content Review and Removal

Who can request a review?

  • The content owner
  • A verified community representative (for Contribution Badges)
  • Any user reporting a violation
  • Law enforcement or regulatory authorities

How to request a review

  • Use the in-app reporting tool
  • Email hello@goauvra.com with "Content Review Request" in the subject line

Review process

  1. We acknowledge receipt within 2-3 business days.
  2. We review the request against this policy and applicable law.
  3. If removal is justified, we remove or restrict access.
  4. We notify the content owner of the outcome. The reporter may also be notified unless doing so would compromise an investigation, violate legal obligations, or breach confidentiality.

Emergency removals

For content that poses immediate risk of harm (e.g., violence, exploitation), we may remove without prior notice and report to authorities.

6. Appeals Process

If your content was removed or your account restricted, you may appeal.

How to appeal

  • Email hello@goauvra.com with "Content Governance Appeal" in the subject line
  • Include: your username, a description of the content or action, and why you believe it should be reconsidered

Appeal process

  1. We review your appeal within 14 days.
  2. We may uphold, reverse, or modify the original decision.
  3. We notify you of the outcome.

Final review

If dissatisfied, you may request a final review by our Compliance Officer (the same role responsible for AML/CFT oversight). The Compliance Officer has authority to review and decide on moderation appeals that raise legal, regulatory, or serious policy concerns. For routine content disputes, a senior moderator will conduct the final review. The decision is final, subject to legal rights.

7. Transparency Reporting

Auvra will publish an annual transparency report covering:

  • Number of content removal requests received
  • Number of removals by category (e.g., copyright, hate speech, cultural misrepresentation)
  • Number of appeals and outcomes
  • Number of accounts suspended or terminated

The first report will be published within 12 months of launch and annually thereafter.

8. Relationship to Other Policies

This Content Governance Policy focuses on verification, moderation, and removal. It does not replace:

  • Copyright Policy - for legal takedowns of infringing material
  • Community Guidelines - for user behaviour rules
  • Content Ownership Policy - for ownership of creator works vs Contribution Badges
  • AI Policy - for Lens AI outputs
  • Collaboration Hub Terms - for collaborative project governance

In the event of a direct conflict, this policy will govern content moderation decisions.
