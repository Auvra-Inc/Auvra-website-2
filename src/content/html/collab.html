1. Introduction

The Collaboration Hub is a feature of the Auvra Platform that allows users to work together on projects including shared workspaces, task management, and agreement tools. These Collaboration Hub Terms ("Hub Terms") supplement our main Terms of Service and apply whenever you use the Collaboration Hub.

The Collaboration Hub is a tool, not a destination. You may use it to collaborate on projects that result in badges preserved on Auvra, projects that are completed entirely outside the Platform, or projects that never produce a badge at all.

By using the Collaboration Hub, you agree to these Hub Terms. If there is a conflict between these Hub Terms and our main Terms of Service, these Hub Terms will govern your use of the Collaboration Hub.

2. Eligibility and Verification

To use the Collaboration Hub, you must complete identity verification (KYC) to the level required by our AML/CFT Policy. Auvra will not grant access to the Collaboration Hub until verification is complete.

Auvra reserves the right to suspend or revoke Collaboration Hub access if your verification status changes or if you are found to have provided false information.

3. Creating or Joining a Collaboration

When you create or join a collaboration, you agree to:

  • Define the scope and goals of the project
  • Identify all collaborators (by Auvra username)
  • Set ownership splits, revenue shares, and usage rights before any work begins (where applicable)
  • Comply with the Community Guidelines and all other Auvra policies

You may invite any verified Auvra user to join a collaboration. Invitations may be accepted or declined.

Collaborations may be for cultural works, commercial projects, community initiatives, educational content, or any other lawful purpose. The Collaboration Hub does not require that the final work be preserved on Auvra or anywhere else.

4. Ownership and Revenue Splits

Before any work begins, collaborators may agree on (but are not required to):

  • Ownership splits - the percentage of the final work owned by each collaborator
  • Revenue splits - the percentage of proceeds from any future sale received by each collaborator
  • Usage rights - whether the work can be used outside the Platform and under what conditions

If you choose to record these terms in the Collaboration Hub, they become binding once all collaborators have accepted them. If you do not use these tools, you are solely responsible for your own agreements.

5. Locking the Agreement

If you record ownership, revenue, or usage terms in the Collaboration Hub, the agreement becomes locked once all collaborators have accepted it. Locked terms cannot be changed except by unanimous consent of all collaborators, and any such change will be recorded as an amendment.

Locking the agreement is only required if you want Auvra to automatically distribute revenue from badge sales. If your project never involves an Auvra badge sale, locking is optional.

6. Revenue Distribution (Only If Applicable)

If your collaboration results in a badge sale on Auvra, and you have locked a revenue split in the Collaboration Hub, Auvra will distribute proceeds according to that split. Distributions are paid to each collaborator through Auvra's payment provider.

If a collaborator has not completed KYC or cannot receive payouts (e.g., due to sanctions, platform restrictions, or unverified status), Auvra will hold their share of the proceeds until they become eligible. If eligibility is not established within a reasonable time (e.g., 12 months), the funds may be donated to a cultural preservation cause at Auvra's discretion, after reasonable attempts to contact the collaborator.

Auvra deducts any applicable transaction charges (as described in our main Terms of Service) before distribution.

If your project does not involve a badge sale on Auvra, this section does not apply. Auvra has no role in any payments made outside the Platform.

7. Dispute Resolution

Disputes arising from a collaboration (e.g., ownership, attribution, revenue, or breach of any agreement) shall first be resolved through good faith negotiations among the collaborators. If the dispute cannot be resolved within thirty (30) days, it may be submitted to the dispute resolution process described in Section 11 of our main Terms of Service (Governing Law & Dispute Resolution).

Auvra does not arbitrate or mediate disputes. We provide the tools for collaboration but are not a party to your agreements.

The term "community governance mechanisms" refers to informal processes that communities may choose to adopt (e.g., elder review, consensus voting), but these are not provided or enforced by Auvra.

8. Termination and Leaving a Collaboration

Leaving a collaboration

Any collaborator may leave a collaboration at any time. Leaving does not cancel obligations already accepted (e.g., ownership or revenue commitments for work already completed). Leaving does not affect ownership or revenue rights for work completed before departure, unless your locked agreement provides otherwise.

Termination by Auvra

Auvra may suspend or terminate access to the Collaboration Hub if a user violates these Hub Terms, the main Terms of Service, or the Community Guidelines. Termination of access does not affect ownership rights in already-completed work.

Completion or abandonment

Collaborations may be marked as completed by unanimous consent, or as abandoned after six consecutive months of inactivity. "Inactivity" means no logins, file uploads, messages, or other actions by any collaborator within that period. Auvra reserves the right to close abandoned collaborations after providing 30 days' notice to the collaborators' registered email addresses.

9. Auvra's Role

Auvra provides the tools for collaboration but is not a party to your agreements. We do not:

  • Require that your project result in a badge or anything on the Platform
  • Verify the accuracy or fairness of any terms you set
  • Enforce your internal agreements except as necessary to operate the Platform (e.g., distributing revenue according to the splits you set)
  • Arbitrate or mediate disputes between collaborators

We may, however, suspend or terminate access to the Collaboration Hub for violations of our policies.
