About Auvra

The stories we keep today become tomorrow's foundation

The voices we record now will speak to generations not yet born. The culture we preserve is the only inheritance that truly matters.

This is why Auvra exists

We are building the permanent home for human culture: a place for creators, communities and families to preserve what they love, own what they make, and pass it down.

Not as a relic. Not as a file. But as something that lives.

The problem we could not ignore

Around the world, languages are falling silent. With them, songs, rituals, techniques and entire ways of seeing the world disappear.

The platforms we trusted were never built for permanence. They were built for engagement. For algorithms. For fleeting attention.

Creators rarely keep control of what they make. Families have nowhere private to keep what matters most. When a platform ends, the culture ends with it.

We refused to accept this.

Our mission

To preserve, structure and empower culture by giving creators, communities and institutions the tools to own and pass down their heritage.

Our vision

To become the global home where human culture is permanently kept, intelligently understood and passed from generation to generation.

How we achieve this

We are building the permanent home where culture is preserved, structured, owned and passed down across generations.

Permanent, verifiable records

Community verification

Storage built to last

Our approach

Co-created with communities

We work alongside cultural custodians, elders and diaspora groups. Transparent, respectful and accountable by design.

Built for the real world

Auvra is mobile-first and designed for low-connectivity environments. Your family Vault is private by default.

Designed for permanence

Not engagement. Not algorithms. Not speculation. What you preserve today is built to outlast us all.

What guides us

Our mission is powered by our C.U.L.T.U.R.E. values. These principles are the foundation of our product, our community and our vision for heritage that lasts.

  • C

    Cultural Integrity

    We treat cultural heritage with the utmost respect and reverence. We prioritise authentic storytelling, verifiable provenance and ethical representation in everything we do.

  • U

    Universal Access

    We believe the tools to preserve and celebrate culture should be available to all. We break down technological barriers with simple design that works on everyday phones.

  • L

    Lasting Stewardship

    We are builders for the long term. We create sustainable models for creators and design records meant to be cherished and preserved for generations to come.

  • T

    Trust Through Transparency

    We operate with honesty and clarity. From onboarding to every record you preserve, we build trust by being open about our processes and holding ourselves to the highest ethical standards.

  • U

    User Empowerment

    We design with deep empathy for our users. We help creators own their narrative and their work, and we help families and communities discover, connect and support one another.

  • R

    Respectful Collaboration

    We believe in the power of community. We foster meaningful connections between creators, families and communities, and work together to build something that benefits everyone.

  • E

    Excellence in Craft

    We honour culture through excellence. We pursue beautiful, museum-quality design in every detail of the product, from the narrative to the interface.

Sustainable Development Goals

How Auvra contributes to the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

SDG 11

Sustainable Cities & Communities

Target 11.4: Protect and safeguard cultural and natural heritage

Auvra preserves endangered cultural heritage, including oral histories, languages and rituals.

SDG 8

Decent Work & Economic Growth

Targets 8.3 & 8.5: Promote decent work for creative professionals

Auvra helps creators keep their intellectual property and be credited for their work.

SDG 10

Reduced Inequalities

Target 10.2: Promote the social and economic inclusion of all

Auvra serves indigenous communities, endangered-language speakers and diaspora families who are often left out of digital preservation.
